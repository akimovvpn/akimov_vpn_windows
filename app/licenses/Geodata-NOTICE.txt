Routing databases shipped in the core folder

geoip.metadb and GeoSite.dat are unmodified copies of the files published by
MetaCubeX/meta-rules-dat (https://github.com/MetaCubeX/meta-rules-dat,
"latest" release, GPL-3.0), downloaded when this app was built. Their SHA-256
checksums are recorded in core/manifest.json.

geoip.metadb is derived from Loyalsoldier/geoip
(https://github.com/Loyalsoldier/geoip, CC BY-SA 4.0), which includes GeoLite2
data created by MaxMind, available from https://www.maxmind.com.

GeoSite.dat is derived from Loyalsoldier/v2ray-rules-dat
(https://github.com/Loyalsoldier/v2ray-rules-dat) and
v2fly/domain-list-community (https://github.com/v2fly/domain-list-community, MIT).

The full GPL-3.0 text is in mihomo-LICENSE.txt in this folder.

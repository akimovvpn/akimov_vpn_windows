akimov vpn 1.0.5 for Windows 10/11 x64.

- The app now uses the new account service at akimov.bid. Sessions from the previous service do not carry over: sign in once after updating.
- Sign in with Telegram: the sign-in screen offers "Continue with Telegram", which opens Telegram's own sign-in page in a separate window, the same sign-in the cabinet uses.
- The window opens straight away from what is saved on this computer; the account refreshes afterwards instead of holding the startup screen.
- When the account service answers only through the VPN, later requests go that way first instead of waiting for the direct route to time out.
- Legal documents that the service has not published yet are left out instead of showing an error.

Choose setup.exe to install, or extract the portable ZIP with its core folder. In-app updates need the installed version. The installer is not code-signed for Windows SmartScreen; update packages are signed and verified by the app. Your other VPN is not disabled by this update.

Bundled core/driver/asset licenses and the core patch are included. The matching upstream core source is included with the release.
